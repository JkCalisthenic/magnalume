# Surface brief: Magnalume landing

Scope: single landing page, Persuade. Audience: chalk athletes who train in poor light, first the co-founder. Action: join the first-batch notice list. Proof: live lamp demo and the product render, no invented claims. Constraints: Spanish copy, logo palette, static hosting.

The owner told us not to ask anything, so the interview and decision page were skipped and the roll stood. Code-led: no photoreal image generator exists here.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A mineral fluorescence room, not a neon product hero. Specimens wait in blackout until you switch the lamp.
OWN-WORLD: Blackout ground, violet only as light, lime only as emission plus one action. Bone specimen labels that turn optical-blue under UV. One white-or-UV rocker switch governs the page. One lime drenched field. Archivo italic expanded.
STORY: You hold the lamp, chalk appears, you understand the product, you ask for the first batch.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL rock. Left, huge headline and CTA. Right, a handprint revealed by the cursor lamp, specimen label bottom right.
FORM: Fourth of seven grounded candidates, seed ef3f4b0c. Raises: emission-line rail, colour only as emission; Saul Bass, one total field; brick instructions, few words per step.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the cursor is the lamp, and the rocker strikes the room to white light with a fluorescent flicker.
