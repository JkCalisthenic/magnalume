# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: the owner said to decide everything. Static HTML/CSS/JS with raw WebGL2, no build step and no framework dependency, deployed on GitHub Pages so a public link can be shared at once.

## Users

Athletes who use chalk (magnesium carbonate) and train where the light is poor: climbers and boulderers, calisthenics athletes at outdoor bars, and anyone in a chalk sport. First audience of this page: the founder's friend and co-founder, then early athletes. (Inferred from the brief; the owner declined the interview and told us to proceed.)

## Product Purpose

Magnalume is sports chalk that glows under ultraviolet light. With a UV lamp, a chalked surface becomes visible in the dark, so the athlete can see where to grip. The product does not exist on the market yet; this page presents the idea and collects interest.

## Positioning

The only chalk you can see in the dark. Ordinary chalk disappears when the light goes; Magnalume shows up under UV.

## Operating Context

Evening or night sessions: outdoor bars with weak street lighting, bouldering at dusk, dim gyms. The athlete chalks up, grips, and uses a UV lamp to see the surface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed: glows under UV light; aimed at sports that use chalk; green powder; black faceted jar with a screw lid and a side window showing the powder (from the product render).
- Undecided, must not be invented: price, launch date, formula, skin and environmental safety, lamp wavelength, whether a lamp is included, whether it keeps glowing after the lamp goes off, gym and crag acceptance.
- No sales channel and no waitlist backend yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Magnalume. Tagline, in English: "Your strength leaves a mark."
- Logo: heavy oblique wordmark with a lime slash through the M and a lime final e. Palette from the logo: near-black and lime on light grey.
- Web copy in Spanish (Spain), direct, gym-floor voice.

## Product Principles

- Show the glow, never just describe it.
- Prove with the visitor's hand on the lamp.
- Say nothing the product has not earned: no specs, prices or safety claims until they are real.
- Loud in one idea, quiet everywhere else.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, reduced-motion support, and everything must read correctly with WebGL or the lamp unavailable.
